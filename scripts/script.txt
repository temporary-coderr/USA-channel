Why does everyone like the guy who makes mistakes?

Because perfect people are annoying.

Psychology calls this the Pratfall Effect.

If you are smart, talented, and perfect all the time, people secretly get jealous of you. They don't like you; they respect you from a distance.

But the moment you make one small, silly mistake—you trip, spill coffee, or forget a word—suddenly everyone thinks, "Oh, he's human like us."

You become likable.

This is proven.

In 1966, Dr. Elliot Aronson did an experiment. He played two recordings. One was a brilliant student answering everything perfectly. The second was the same brilliant student, but he spilled coffee on himself at the end.

Guess what? People liked the second guy way more. Same intelligence, just one small mistake.

So stop trying to be perfect in front of people. Perfect is boring. Perfect is scary.

Be good at what you do, but let people see your small flaws. That's what makes you real.

That one mistake you're embarrassed about? That might be the reason people actually like you.
