You walk into a room... and suddenly forget WHY you came here.

It happens to many of us. And there's a fascinating explanation.

It's called the Doorway Effect.

Your brain constantly updates its understanding of what's happening around you. A doorway can act like a mental event boundary, separating one experience from the next.

In a 2011 study, University of Notre Dame researchers tested this in real and virtual environments.

People remembered less after passing through a doorway than after travelling the same distance without crossing one.

So next time you forget why you entered a room, don't panic.

Your brain may just be updating the scene.
