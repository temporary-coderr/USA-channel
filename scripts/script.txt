Your  dog does not love you for your food. He loves you like a child loves a parent.

And this is not just a cute line. This is science.

When you look into your dog's eyes, and your dog looks back at you, both of your brains release the same hormone that a mother releases when she holds her baby. It's called Oxytocin. The love hormone.

In 2015, researchers in Japan proved this. They found that when a dog and its owner gaze at each other, both of their oxytocin levels go up. Just by looking. No food. No play. Just eye contact.

That's why when you come home after a long day, your dog doesn't just wag his tail. His whole body shakes. Because for him, you were gone for a lifetime.

Humans have friends, jobs, phones, a whole world. But for your dog, you ARE the whole world.

He doesn't care if you are rich or poor. He doesn't care if you failed today. He doesn't care if you look tired. He just wants 5 minutes with you.

We say we rescue dogs. But the truth is, most of the time, they rescue us. From loneliness. From stress. From feeling unloved.

So today, when you go home, don't just feed him. Sit with him for 2 minutes. Look into his eyes. He has been waiting for that moment all day.
